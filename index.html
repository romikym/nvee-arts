The artist

Meet Veronica.

NVee Arts is the studio of Veronica DeSio — a North Hollywood artist working at the intersection of fluid acrylic, resin, and hand-painted mixed media. Every piece in this shop was poured, painted, or sealed by her in a small home studio in the 818.

She paints what makes her feel something — Dodger blue, Raider silver, a single rose, the word love written in twelve different colors. Some pieces are ready-to-hang canvases. Others are mini resin tiles small enough to sit on a shelf.

No two pieces are alike. Most take days to dry. All of them are one of one, signed on the back, and shipped from North Hollywood.

Process

Pour. Wait.
Watch it move.

Fluid art doesn't follow rules. Veronica mixes acrylic with pouring medium and a few drops of silicone, tilts the canvas, and lets gravity do the rest. Cells appear. Rivers form. Each piece is a single, unrepeatable moment — then sealed in resin so the colors stay alive forever.

Custom commissions open

Want something made just for you?

Veronica takes a handful of commissions each month — anniversaries, wedding gifts, team logos, custom color palettes for a specific room. Most pieces range from $85–$350 depending on size and complexity, with a 2–4 week turnaround.

Frequently asked

Quick answers.

How long does shipping take?
Every order ships from North Hollywood within 2–3 business days via USPS Priority Mail. You'll receive a tracking number by email the moment it ships. Domestic delivery is typically 2–4 business days from there.
Do you ship internationally?
Not through the standard checkout yet, but yes — by request. Email or DM with your address and the piece you want and Veronica will send a custom shipping quote, usually within 24 hours.
Are returns accepted?
Because every piece is one of a kind, returns are accepted only if the piece arrives damaged. Photos within 48 hours of delivery and a full refund or replacement is issued. Custom commissions are final sale.
Can I commission a custom piece?
Absolutely. Use the form above with "Custom commission" selected, or DM @nveearts. Be ready to share: rough size, color preferences, the room or occasion, and your timeline. Veronica replies with a quote and process within 24 hours.
Can I see pieces in person before buying?
Veronica does occasional local pop-ups around the Valley and LA. Subscribe to the newsletter below or follow @nveearts for dates. Private studio visits are available by appointment for serious collectors.
